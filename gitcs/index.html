Great Ideas in Theoretical Computer Science

Theory Lunch Edition!

If you are not on the mailing list, sign up here.
Where: G5 Lounge
When: Wednesdays at 12:45 PM

Theoretical Computer Science is full of great ideas, so once a week, the MIT Theory group gets together to socialize and share some knowledge. We'll briefly share some interesting ideas, and discuss a superset of these ideas over lunch. If you have any suggestions for future discussions, please contact g@csail.mit.edu.

This page is for historical purposes - for the current Theory Lunch website, please go here.
