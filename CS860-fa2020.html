CS 860 - Algorithms for Private Data Analysis - Fall 2020

Course Description

This course is on algorithms for differentially private analysis of data. As necessitated by the nature of differential privacy, this course will be theoretically and mathematically based. References to practice will be provided as relevant, especially towards the end of the course. Prerequisites include an undergraduate understanding of algorithms, comfort with probability, and mathematical maturity.

Grading

Grades for this course will be determined with the following breakdown:
  • Weekly Quizzes: 5%. These will consist of one or two easy questions, just to make sure you're keeping up with the content.
  • Two Assignments: 25%. These will be mathematical/proof based.
  • Paper Summary: 30%. You will be required to carefully and critically read a paper, and provide a "lecture notes" or "expository-focused" style summary. There will be a proposal (not graded) and a final submission. More details forthcoming. Some of the best submissions may be invited to be refined into a post on DifferentialPrivacy.org.
  • Final Project: 40%. A project may be theoretical, experimental, or a mix of the two. There will be a proposal (not graded) and a final submission. More details forthcoming.
