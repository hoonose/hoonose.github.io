Gautam Kamath is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at the Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences at New York University. He is also a Faculty Member at the Vector Institute, and on leave from the University of Waterloo. He has a B.S. from Cornell University, and a Ph.D. from MIT. He is interested in reliable and trustworthy algorithms, statistics, and machine learning, including considerations such as data privacy and robustness. He was a postdoc at the Simons Institute for the Theory of Computing. He serves as an Editor-in-Chief of Transactions on Machine Learning Research, and was the program committee co-chair of ALT 2025. He serves in the governance of ICML, COLT, ALT, and FORC, and is the steering committee chair of SaTML. He is the recipient of several awards, including the Presburger Award, the Gödel Prize, the Caspar Bowden Award for Outstanding Research in Privacy Enhancing Technologies, a best paper award at ICML 2024, a FOCS Test of Time Award, and an Ontario Early Researcher Award.
